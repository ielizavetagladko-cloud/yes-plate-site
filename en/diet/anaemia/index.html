Iron-deficiency anaemia: diet, what you can and cannot eat

Food cannot cure anaemia — iron tablets and a blood test do. What food can do is help: iron on the plate, and vitamin C beside it, because it is what lets plant iron through. The daily guideline is 16 mg for women before menopause and 11 mg for men and after it.

What a dish should not contain

Nothing is forbidden here — there is only what to aim for.

What to watch for

The dish is not ruled out, but you get a warning if it contains:

  • caffeine

What there should be enough of

This is not a ban. The app flags a dish that has too little of it — it does no harm, it just does not work for you.

  • Iron — at least 3.5 mg

Guidelines for the day

  • Iron — at least 16 mg
