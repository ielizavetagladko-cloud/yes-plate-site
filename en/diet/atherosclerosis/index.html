High cholesterol: diet, what you can and cannot eat

What moves the numbers is saturated fat, not the cholesterol in food itself — frying, fatty meat and offal, which is high in both. Eggs and prawns were restricted for decades for the cholesterol they contain, and the evidence for that turned out to be weak. Soluble fibre works the other way: oats, beans, apples lower LDL modestly but dependably, which is why the app asks a dish for some. Oily fish helps with triglycerides, and alcohol raises them.

What a dish should not contain

A dish does not fit if it contains:

  • organ meat
  • fried food

What to watch for

The dish is not ruled out, but you get a warning if it contains:

  • red meat
  • a lot of fat
  • alcohol
  • a lot of salt

Per-portion limits

These are the numbers the app checks every dish against.

  • Fat — up to 15 g
  • Sodium — up to 450 mg

What there should be enough of

This is not a ban. The app flags a dish that has too little of it — it does no harm, it just does not work for you.

  • Fibre — at least 4 g

Guidelines for the day

  • Fat — up to 65 g
  • Sodium — up to 2000 mg
