Diets for 14 conditions

Each condition restricts something different: one salt, another gluten, a third fat. Here is what should stay off the plate for each of them, and what is left to cook. The numbers are the ones Yes Plate checks 178 recipes against.

  • Ulcer & gastritisA sore stomach lining reacts to anything sharp, sour or fried. Food should be gentle, warm and soft.
  • DiabetesKeeping blood sugar steady means limiting fast sugars and choosing slower carbohydrates.
  • Coeliac diseaseGluten damages the gut even in tiny amounts, so wheat, rye and barley are out completely.
  • Lactose intoleranceMilk sugar is not digested and causes bloating and pain. Plant-based substitutes work well.
  • High blood pressureSalt raises blood pressure, so herbs and lemon replace the salt shaker.
  • Kidney diseaseWeakened kidneys struggle to clear salt, potassium and protein waste, so all three are limited.
  • Pancreas & gallbladderFat is the main trigger of an attack. Meals should be lean, steamed or baked, and small.

  • IBS (low FODMAP)Certain carbohydrates ferment in the gut and cause cramps. Onion, garlic and wheat are the usual culprits.
