Diabetes: diet, what you can and cannot eat

Keeping blood sugar steady means limiting fast sugars and choosing slower carbohydrates.

What a dish should not contain

A dish does not fit if it contains:

  • a lot of sugar

What to watch for

The dish is not ruled out, but you get a warning if it contains:

  • a lot of fat
  • fried food
