Kidney disease: diet, what you can and cannot eat

Weakened kidneys struggle to clear salt, potassium and protein waste, so all three are limited.

What a dish should not contain

A dish does not fit if it contains:

  • a lot of salt
  • a lot of potassium

What to watch for

The dish is not ruled out, but you get a warning if it contains:

  • a lot of protein

Per-portion limits

These are the numbers the app checks every dish against.

  • Sodium — up to 400 mg
  • Potassium — up to 600 mg
  • Protein — up to 15 g

Guidelines for the day

  • Sodium — up to 2000 mg
  • Potassium — up to 2500 mg
