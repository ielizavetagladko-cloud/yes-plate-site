Vegan: diet, what you can and cannot eat

Nothing from animals at all: no meat, fish, dairy, eggs or honey. B12 comes only from supplements — food cannot cover it.

What a dish should not contain

A dish does not fit if it contains:

  • fish
  • dairy
  • eggs
  • honey
